# Awesome AI Tools 🛠️

A hand-picked, plain-English list of tools for building and using AI — what each one is, and a link to the source. Curated by [NeLocker](https://nelocker.com). All entries are links + our own summaries; all rights remain with their owners.

> Open an issue or PR to suggest a tool. We keep this current.

## Run models on your own machine
- **[Ollama](https://ollama.com)** — the easiest way to download and run open AI models locally, privately, and offline.
- **[llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)** — run language models efficiently on your own hardware, even modest machines.

## Build AI into your app
- **[Vercel AI SDK](https://sdk.vercel.ai)** — a toolkit for chat, structured output, tool calling, and streaming across providers.
- **[Anthropic SDK](https://github.com/anthropics/anthropic-sdk-typescript)** — official libraries for building with Claude.

## Connect AI to your tools & data
- **[Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://modelcontextprotocol.io)** — an open standard for securely connecting AI assistants to tools, files, and data.

## Personal AI assistants (open frameworks)
- **[OpenJarvis](https://github.com/open-jarvis/OpenJarvis)** — Stanford SAIL's local-first, open-source framework for a personal AI agent that runs on your hardware.

## Voice & speech
- **[Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper)** — an open speech-to-text model; turn your voice into text.

## Build the interface
- **[shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com)** — copy-paste React components you own and customize.
- **[Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com)** — utility-first styling that pairs well with AI-built UIs.

## Images, video & media
- **[ComfyUI](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI)** — a powerful, node-based, open interface for AI image/video workflows.

## Background jobs & automation
- **[Inngest](https://www.inngest.com)** — durable background jobs and workflows, great for AI pipelines.
