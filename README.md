# NeLocker Open 🔐

> Free, open resources for getting more out of AI — curated by the team behind [NeLocker](https://nelocker.com), *Your Intelligence Vault*.

Everything here is **free and openly licensed (MIT / CC0)**. Use it, fork it, build on it. If it helps you, a ⭐ means a lot.

## What's inside

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**`/prompts`**](./prompts) | A curated set of original, ready-to-use AI prompts — each with what it does + how to use it. Works with any model (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, …). |
| [**`awesome-ai-tools.md`**](./awesome-ai-tools.md) | A hand-picked, plain-English list of the best tools for building with AI — assistants, local models, app frameworks, voice, and more. |
| [**`/legal`**](./legal) | A practical compliance checklist + copy-and-customize legal templates (Privacy, Terms, Cookies) for launching a site or app. *Educational, not legal advice.* |

## Why we made this open

We believe the right words can unlock something in anyone — and that good tools and good information shouldn't be locked behind a paywall to *learn from*. The paid NeLocker product is the curated, organized vault and the platform around it. **This** is our gift to the community: the open foundation.

## Philosophy

We're neither AI hype nor AI doom. We believe in **truth, understanding, and using these tools responsibly** — it was never the tool that's the problem, it's how it's used. ([Read our full stance.](https://nelocker.com/responsible-ai))

## Contributing

Suggestions and PRs welcome — new prompts, better tool entries, fixes. Keep everything **original or openly licensed with attribution preserved**; we don't include leaked or proprietary content.
