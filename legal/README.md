## The checklist

**Privacy Policy** — publish one before collecting any data; say what you collect, why, how long, and who you share with; offer access/deletion + a contact. *Don't* collect "just in case" or hide the policy.

**Terms of Service** — cover acceptable use, billing, disclaimers, limitation of liability, IP, termination, governing law; require agreement. *Don't* promise outcomes you can't guarantee.

**Cookies (EU ePrivacy)** — show a consent banner for non-essential cookies; get consent *before* setting them; let users reject as easily as accept. *Don't* pre-tick boxes or fire analytics before consent.

**GDPR / UK GDPR** — have a lawful basis; honor access/erasure/portability/objection rights; sign DPAs with vendors; use safeguards for US transfers; breach-notify within 72 hours. *Don't* assume GDPR doesn't apply to a small US startup if you target EU users.

**US — California (CCPA/CPRA) & state laws** — give notice + rights to know/delete/correct/opt-out; add a "Do Not Sell or Share" link if applicable; watch the growing patchwork (Virginia, Colorado, Texas, …). *Don't* assume "US = no privacy law."

**Children (COPPA / under-16)** — state plainly you're not directed to under-13s and don't knowingly collect their data; avoid being "child-directed" unless ready for the obligations. *Don't* market to or profile children.

**Email (CAN-SPAM / CASL / GDPR)** — working unsubscribe in every marketing email, honored promptly; accurate from/subject + physical address; consent for EU/Canada. *Don't* buy lists.

**Payments & security** — use a processor (Stripe) so you never touch card data; HTTPS; never expose secret keys client-side; have a breach plan. *Don't* store cards or commit secrets.

**Accessibility (ADA / WCAG)** — aim for WCAG 2.1 AA (alt text, keyboard nav, contrast, labels). *Don't* ignore it — web accessibility lawsuits are common.

**IP & content** — only use assets you own or are licensed to use (preserve attribution); publish a DMCA process. *Don't* use copyrighted/"found" assets without a license.

**Cover yourself** — footer links to Privacy/Terms/Cookies/Contact site-wide; keep records of consent; form an entity; get insurance; have a lawyer review before launch.

## Templates

> Customize every **[BRACKET]** for your business. Starting points, not legal advice.

### Privacy Policy (template)
```markdown
# Privacy Policy — [COMPANY NAME]
_Effective date: [DATE]_

[COMPANY NAME] ("we") operates [WEBSITE]. This explains how we handle personal data.

## Information we collect
- Account data (e.g., name, email).
- Payment data — handled by [PROCESSOR]; we don't store card details.
- Technical data — IP, device/browser, cookies.

## How we use it
To provide the service, process payments, send transactional email, and (with consent) marketing. To secure the service and comply with law.

## Sharing
Only with service providers under contract. We do not sell your personal data.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) you may access, correct, delete, port, and object. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL].

## Cookies / Children / Security
See our Cookie Policy. Not directed to children under 13. We use industry-standard security.

## Contact
[COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS] — [PRIVACY EMAIL].
```

### Terms of Service (template)
```markdown
# Terms of Service — [COMPANY NAME]
_Last updated: [DATE]_

By using [WEBSITE] (the "Service") you agree to these Terms.

## Eligibility & accounts
You must be 18+ (or have guardian consent). You're responsible for your account.

## Payment
[Pricing/billing]. Subscriptions [auto-renew]. See refund policy.

## Acceptable use / IP
Don't misuse, break the law, or infringe rights. We own the Service; you keep your content and license us to display it.

## Disclaimers & liability
PROVIDED "AS IS." Liability limited to what you paid in the prior 12 months, where permitted.

## Governing law & contact
Governed by [STATE/COUNTRY]. [COMPANY NAME] — [SUPPORT EMAIL].
```

### Cookie Policy (template)
```markdown
# Cookie Policy — [COMPANY NAME]
_Last updated: [DATE]_

- **Essential** (always on): sign-in and core function.
- **Optional** (consent required): [analytics], set only if you accept.

Manage choices via our banner or your browser. Some providers (e.g., [PROCESSOR]) set their own cookies.
Contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL].
```
